// 01. about

Curious by nature.
Offensive by trade.

I'm an offensive security analyst based in Bengaluru. Day to day I run penetration tests against the systems organizations rely on most — web apps, APIs, cloud (Azure / Entra ID, AWS, GCP), Active Directory, and increasingly the LLM-powered features getting bolted onto all of them.

At Casepoint I run monthly, iteration-based assessments in IL4–6 environments — over 18 consecutive months I've validated 100+ vulnerabilities and driven 80%+ of them to a fix within two sprints. I like the part after the finding as much as the finding itself: reproducing it cleanly, scoring real impact, and getting a developer to actually close it.

Lately a lot of my time goes into AI security — testing chatbots and RAG features for prompt injection and access-control bugs, and building my own tooling on top of Claude (wired into Burp over MCP) to cut validation time. When I step to the other side, I fold red-team tradecraft and MITRE ATT&CK into engagements to pressure-test detection, not just find bugs.

Outside of testing I'm usually writing Python/PowerShell tooling, publishing internal security notes, or chipping away at the next cert.
